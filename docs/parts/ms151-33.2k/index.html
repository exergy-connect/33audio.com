What is MS151-33.2K?

Caddock MS151-33.2K is a 33.2 kΩ Type MS151 Micronox film resistor. It is NOS: these resistors are liquidation stock from a Caddock stocking distributor that closed in the 1980s. The price is $10.00, and 33audio ships it from Houston, TX.

What is the difference between Caddock Type MM and Type MS?

Type MM, including MM177 and MM215, is Caddock's precision film series in a molded body. Type MS, including MS151 and MS176, is the power film series with a silicone conformal coating. Both use Micronox film. Caddock marks Type MM Not Recommended for New Designs and points new designs to Type MS. Those distinctions are on the 1985 datasheet scans and on Caddock's current Type MM datasheet.

What does NOS mean for these resistors?

On this site, NOS means the liquidation stock described by 33audio: These resistors are liquidation stock from a Caddock stocking distributor that closed in the 1980s. It is not a claim that Caddock still recommends the part for a new design.

What are these Caddock resistors used for?

The 1985 Caddock datasheet scans linked from this site describe these Micronox film resistors for high-temperature work in geophysical, industrial, aerospace, and military equipment, and the 33audio listing calls them ideal for harsh environments. Temperature Range to 275 deg/C max. Buyers also use exact NOS values to repair equipment that already has a Caddock MM or MS resistor in it.
